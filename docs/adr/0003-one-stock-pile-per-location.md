# One stock pile per product per location, no stock purposes

The first plan split stock by product + location + purpose (production, sale, güvence), with güvence physically locked until an engineer released it. Nobody at Artek physically separates stock by purpose, so every borrow between purposes would need a "purpose change" entry that people would skip, which is exactly how the current notebook/Excel numbers lost trust. We keep one pile per product per location; what stock is for comes from the product (Ticari mal, Malzeme) and from Rezervasyon. Güvence is replaced by a Kritik seviye that warns and never blocks.

## Consequences

Holding stock back for a future need is done with a Rezervasyon, not a separate pile. Reintroducing purposes later would mean re-counting and splitting every balance.
